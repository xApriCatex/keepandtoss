Privacy policy

For the Keep & Toss iPhone app. Last updated 3 November 2026.

The short version

Your photos never leave your iPhone. There is no account, and we don't collect, see, sell or share any data.

Who we are

Keep & Toss is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: dimroom.app@outlook.com.

Your photos

The app reads your photo library on the phone, with your permission, only to show you your photos and to delete the ones you choose. Photos are never uploaded, copied off the phone or analysed by anyone. Deleting always goes through Apple's own confirmation and moves photos to Recently Deleted.

What the app keeps on your phone

Which photos you marked keep or toss, how many free swipes you have used today, and whether you bought Unlimited. These stay on your iPhone. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

Purchases

Unlimited is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns from Apple whether you own Unlimited. We never see your name or payment details.

Deleting your data

Use Start over in Settings, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Keep & Toss collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Your rights

Questions about privacy: dimroom.app@outlook.com. In the UK you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
