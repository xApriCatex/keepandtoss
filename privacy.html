Privacy policy

For the Keep & Toss iPhone app. Last updated 16 November 2026.

The short version

Your photos never leave your iPhone. There is no account, and we don't collect, see, sell or share any data.

Who we are

Keep & Toss is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: dimroom.app@outlook.com.

Your photos

The app reads your photo library on the phone, with your permission, to show you your photos, to delete the ones you choose, and, when you use Smart Clear, to find similar shots, copies, and blurry or dark photos. Smart Clear's checks run on your iPhone using Apple's own on-device tools: they measure how sharp and bright each photo is, compare photos with each other, and look at the quality of any faces in a set of similar shots to suggest the best one. They never recognise who anyone is. Photos are never uploaded or copied off the phone, and no person sees them. Deleting always goes through Apple's own confirmation and moves photos to Recently Deleted.

What the app keeps on your phone

Which photos you marked keep or toss, how many free swipes you have used recently, whether you bought Unlimited, and Smart Clear's measurements of your photos (so the next scan is quick). For burst photos, Smart Clear also keeps a list of the extra frames in each burst (their photo IDs and dates) and small preview pictures of those frames, so it can show them to you; previews not used for a week are removed the next time Smart Clear opens (and iPhone may clear them sooner when space runs low). If a saved list of your choices is ever damaged, the app keeps the damaged copy aside so nothing is lost. All of this stays on your iPhone (and in your own iPhone backup, if you make one; Smart Clear's measurements and the burst previews are left out of it). The app has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

Purchases

Unlimited is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns from Apple whether you own Unlimited. We never see your name or payment details.

Deleting your data

Start over in Settings forgets your keep and toss choices, including any damaged copy kept aside. Deleting the app removes what it kept, Smart Clear's measurements included, except two small notes iOS keeps in the phone's keychain so a reinstall does not lose them: whether you bought Unlimited, and your recent free-swipe counts. Neither contains anything about your photos. iPhone or iCloud backups made before you delete the app still contain what was in them until those backups are replaced or deleted. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Keep & Toss collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
