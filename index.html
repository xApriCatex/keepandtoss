Keep & Toss

Clear out your iPhone camera roll one photo at a time. Swipe right to keep, left to toss. Nothing is deleted until you review the bin and say so.

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: dimroom.app@outlook.com (the developer's support address for all our apps). Please say which iPhone you have.

Common questions

Is anything deleted when I swipe left? No. Tossed photos wait in the bin. When you tap Delete in the bin, iOS asks you to confirm, and the photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Getting the space back now: open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted, and delete them there.

Undo: the Undo button brings back your last swipes. In the bin, tap any photo to keep it after all.

Only some of my photos show: you gave access to selected photos only. Tap "Choose more" in the app, or go to iPhone Settings, Apps, Keep & Toss, Photos, and pick Full Access.

Free swipes: 100 swipes a day are free, and the count refills at midnight. Deleting is always free.

Unlimited: a one-time purchase for unlimited swipes and the quick sorts (Screenshots, Videos longest first). On a new iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchase".

Starting over: Settings, Start over, forgets your keep and toss choices. It never deletes photos.
